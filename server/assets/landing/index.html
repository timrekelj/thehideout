Invite-only · made for friends

Your own cozy corner of the internet.

thehideout is a small chat app for a group of friends: servers and channels, direct messages, voice and video, all in a warm, quiet place that is yours.

Everything a friend group needs

No ads, no crowds. Just the people you invited.

  • Servers and channels

    Give every group its own server, with text and voice channels, roles and permissions.

  • Direct messages

    Talk one-on-one or in small groups, right next to your servers.

  • Voice, video, screen share

    Hop into a voice channel, turn on your camera or share your screen.

  • Markdown and custom emoji

    Format messages with markdown, react to them, and add your own emoji to a server.

  • Files and link previews

    Attach images and files, and get a preview of the links you share.

  • Secure sign-in

    Protect your account with passkeys or an authenticator app (two-factor).
